Privacy Policy — Slide Quest

Last updated: October 2, 2026

Overview

Slide Quest (package: com.ziadimad.android.slidequest) is an offline sliding-puzzle game. It does not require an account, and gameplay progress is stored locally on the device.

Advertising and data

Slide Quest displays banner advertisements through Google AdMob. Google Mobile Ads may collect and process IP address, app and advertisement interactions, diagnostic information, and device identifiers such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID. This information may be used for advertising, analytics, security, and fraud prevention.

Google processes this information under the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices

Where required, the app displays a consent message before requesting advertisements. Users can reopen privacy choices from the privacy control shown in the app. Android advertising identifiers can also be reset or deleted through Android device settings.

Children

Slide Quest is intended for users aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact ziadimad.android@gmail.com.
