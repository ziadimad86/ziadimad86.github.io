Privacy Policy — Crystal Valley

Effective date: October 25, 2026

About the game

Crystal Valley is a puzzle game by Ziad Imad (Android package: ziadimad.android.crystalvalley). It is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over. The game does not require an account or ask for your name, email address, contacts, or precise location.

Game data

Game progress, rewards, and settings are stored on your device. We do not operate a server that receives your game progress or an account database for this game.

Advertising data

The game uses Google AdMob to display banner advertisements. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share IP address, app and advertisement interactions, diagnostic information, and device identifiers such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID. Google uses this information for advertising, measurement, and fraud prevention. Advertising may be personalized where permitted by your choices and applicable requirements.

Google handles this information under the Google Privacy Policy. We do not sell your personal information. When required, the game asks for advertising consent before requesting ads and provides a Privacy choices control in Settings when available.

Security, retention, and deletion

Game progress and settings remain on your device until you clear the app's data or uninstall it. Data sent by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit. Google's retention practices are described in its privacy policy. You can reset or delete the Android advertising ID in your device settings.

Children

Crystal Valley is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly request personal information from children.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy if the game or its data practices change. The effective date above will be updated. For privacy questions, contact ziadimad.android@gmail.com.
