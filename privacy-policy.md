# Privacy Policy — English Basics

Effective date: September 29, 2026

English Basics ("the app") helps users learn basic English vocabulary and pronunciation.

## Information we collect
The app does not require an account and does not collect names, email addresses, passwords, contacts, or precise location. Learning progress and settings are stored locally on the device.

The app may display advertisements provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use information such as advertising identifiers, device information, and usage data according to Google's policies. Users can review Google's privacy information at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

## How we use information
We use locally stored settings and progress to provide the app's learning features. Advertising partners may use information to provide and measure advertisements.

## Sharing
We do not sell personal information. Information handled by advertising providers is governed by their own privacy policies.

## Children's privacy
The app is intended for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Security and retention
Because learning data is stored locally, it remains on the device until the user clears the app data or uninstalls the app. We do not operate a server storing user accounts or learning data.

## Changes
We may update this policy when the app or applicable requirements change. The effective date above will be updated when changes are made.
